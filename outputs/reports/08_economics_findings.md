# 08 economics findings

Author: Chanakya

Committed acquisition of INR 1.97 Cr buys 151.2k attributed payers at INR 150 blended attributed CAC including brand and measurement, which meets INR 200 incremental CAC only at 75% incrementality or better. Each acquired payer contributes INR 98.56 over 24 months, so no channel repays its CAC on pass purchases alone; owned is closest. Without upgrades the campaign loses INR 0.99 to 1.59 Cr over 24 months. Against a 10% control upgrade rate among 864k eligible pass buyers, 24-month break-even needs a treatment upgrade rate of 13.9% to 15.7%, and 15-month payback needs 16.9% to 19.0%. The reference case (16%, 87.5%) nets INR 0.50 Cr at 24 months with 17.1-month payback, so it would not pass Gate 3. Scale therefore waits for Gate 2 evidence. The performance reserve of INR 0.77 Cr is released only on measured marginal incremental CAC at or below INR 200.
